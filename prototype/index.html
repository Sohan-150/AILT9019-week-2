Bin-finder

Which bin does this item go in? One question, one answer, cited to a rule.

Prototype, Week 2 · AILT9019. Runs entirely in your browser — no network, no model.

What this does not do

  • Does not handle hazardous-waste disposal — it hands off to building staff.
  • Does not override building staff instructions; their word wins.
  • Does not invent a rule. If the item is not in the published source, it says so.
